What Your Competitors Are Doing

Three agencies I studied. Three lessons.

Each one does something well that Better Lives can learn from — and each has a clear weakness that creates an opening for us.

SYNERGY HomeCare Same Zip Code — Ellicott City
Local SEO Individual pages for every city they serve — Catonsville, Elkridge, Pikesville, Randalstown, Woodstock. A dedicated page for every local search.
Leadership Owner (Doctor of Physical Therapy) and Geriatric Nurse Practitioner named with credentials on the About page.
Google Reviews 4.9 stars with ~35 reviews — the strongest local trust signal in healthcare search.
Content "Knowledge Center" blog publishing monthly on caregiver topics — VA benefits, fall prevention, hospital recovery.
Our Opportunity
Their Gap
Home care only — they cannot place staff into facilities. Their single-sided model caps their growth ceiling.
Our Move
Build location pages for every county and city we serve, capturing their search traffic — while also serving a facility audience they structurally cannot reach.
Maryland Health Care Staffing Content Marketing Leader
Blog Library 15+ posts targeting healthcare staffing keywords, with internal links to role-specific service pages.
Role Pages Separate service pages for each professional role — RN, LPN, CNA, CMT, OT, COTA. Targets the exact terms facilities use to search.
Recruitment Clear worker benefits displayed — direct deposit, competitive pay, flexible hours, 24/7 support. Inline application with resume upload.
Our Opportunity
Their Gap
Blog completely inactive since March 2023 — three years stale. No city or county-specific pages anywhere on the site.
Our Move
Launch a consistent content engine and geo-targeted county pages. We overtake their entire digital strategy within months.
Capital City Nurses Multi-State — MD / VA / DC
Content Depth Service pages run 1,100+ words each with detailed care explanations, sub-services, and FAQ sections.
Branded Process Proprietary "Care360" assessment and a four-step care journey visible on every service page.
Leadership Five named leaders with photos, titles, and detailed bios — from Executive VP of Operations to VP of Human Resources.
Our Opportunity
Their Gap
Blog link returns a 404 error. Care360 is described but not interactive online. No pricing, no insurance information, no live engagement tools.
Our Move
Build a functioning content hub and a real interactive care assessment — experiences none of these three competitors currently offer.
