A Two-Sided Business

Better Lives doesn't just serve families and individuals — it also places qualified healthcare professionals into hospitals, nursing homes, and facilities. That makes it a two-sided marketplace. Most of the competitors I studied only operate on one side. This is a significant structural advantage that the current website doesn't communicate at all.

Section 02 — What Your Competitors Are Doing

Three agencies I studied. Three lessons.

I investigated the top three competitors in and around Howard County. Each one does something well digitally that Better Lives can learn from — and each has a clear weakness that creates an opening for us.

SYNERGY HomeCare Same Zip Code — Ellicott City

SYNERGY has individual web pages for every city they serve — Catonsville, Elkridge, Pikesville, Randalstown, Woodstock. When someone in Catonsville searches "home care near me," SYNERGY has a dedicated page waiting for them. They also feature their owner (Doctor of Physical Therapy) and a nurse practitioner by name with credentials on the About page. They have 4.9 stars on Google with around 35 reviews, a blog publishing monthly, and a live chat widget for instant responses.

Our Opportunity Better Lives covers more counties than SYNERGY but has zero location-specific pages. We can build dedicated pages for every county and city we serve — capturing the search traffic SYNERGY and others are currently winning.
Maryland Health Care Staffing Content Marketing Leader

MHS has 15+ blog posts targeting healthcare staffing keywords and individual service pages for each professional role — RN, LPN, CNA, CMT, OT. Their careers page lists specific job roles with benefits clearly stated: direct deposit, competitive pay, flexible hours, 24/7 support. They post regularly on Facebook and Instagram using video content.

Our Opportunity Their entire blog has been inactive since 2023 — three years without new content. If we launch a consistent content engine, we overtake their full library within months. Their role-specific service pages are smart — we should build the same for the staffing side of the business.
Capital City Nurses Multi-State — MD / VA / DC

Capital City Nurses is part of a larger parent company (Care Advantage). Their service pages run over 1,100 words each with detailed process explanations. They've branded their intake system as "Care360" — a structured assessment tool. Five named leaders with photos and bios on the About page. Industry award badges displayed prominently. Proper technical SEO implemented throughout.

Our Opportunity Their blog link is completely broken and returns an error page. They have no interactive tools online despite advertising them. If we build a functioning content hub and a real care needs assessment, we offer experiences none of these competitors currently deliver.
